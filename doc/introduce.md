## 🌈 Full Description

🎉 Light up the board and let the combos fly in **Neon Jewels**, a vibrant match-3 adventure packed with sparkling gems, explosive cascades, and satisfying challenges!

💥 Swap and match colorful jewels, create powerful special gems, and plan spectacular chain reactions. Every move can trigger a shower of neon effects—and every level brings a fresh puzzle to solve!

🌌 **Explore a glowing galaxy**

Journey through **200 handcrafted levels across 10 neon worlds**. Chase high scores, collect colors, clear jelly, beat the clock, rescue ingredients, and smash tricky obstacles. Watch out for conveyor belts, portals, gravity streams, cages, spreading jam, countdown bombs, shifting walls, and many more clever twists!

🎮 **Pick your favorite challenge**

- 👾 Battle mighty Neon Bosses
- 🌀 Flip the entire board in Gravity mode
- 🎵 Match to the beat in Rhythm mode
- 🔥 Push your limits in Endless and Survival
- 🌈 Build sizzling streaks in Color Rush
- 🥤 Raise bottles through bubbling Soda boards
- 🧩 Solve limited-move Puzzles and shifting Labyrinths
- 📅 Discover a fresh Daily Challenge
- 🧘 Relax with no pressure in Zen mode
- ⚔️ Challenge a friend in local Versus battles

🎁 **More rewards, more reasons to play**

Complete daily quests, climb the Battle Pass, compete in seasonal leagues, unlock achievements and titles, collect stickers, improve your progression tree, spin the lucky wheel, and personalize your adventure with dazzling gem skins and board themes.
