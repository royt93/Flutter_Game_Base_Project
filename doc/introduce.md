## Full description

Light up the board and let the combos fly in Neon Jewels, a vibrant match-3 adventure packed with sparkling gems, explosive cascades, and satisfying challenges!

Swap and match colorful jewels, create powerful special gems, and plan spectacular chain reactions. Every move can trigger a shower of neon effects—and every level brings a fresh puzzle to solve.

Explore 200 handcrafted levels across 10 glowing worlds. Chase high scores, collect colors, clear jelly, beat the clock, rescue ingredients, and smash tricky obstacles. As your journey unfolds, you will face conveyor belts, portals, gravity streams, cages, spreading jam, countdown bombs, shifting walls, and many more clever twists.

Want a different kind of challenge? Take on a huge variety of game modes:

• Battle mighty Neon Bosses
• Flip the board in Gravity mode
• Match to the beat in Rhythm mode
• Push your limits in Endless and Survival
• Build hot-color streaks in Color Rush
• Raise bottles through bubbling Soda boards
• Solve limited-move Puzzles and shifting Labyrinths
• Discover a new Daily Challenge
• Relax with no pressure in Zen mode
• Challenge a friend in local Versus battles

The fun keeps growing beyond the board. Complete daily quests, climb the Battle Pass, compete in seasonal leagues, unlock achievements and titles, collect stickers, improve your progression tree, spin the lucky wheel, and personalize the game with dazzling gem skins and board themes.

Why you will love Neon Jewels:

• Fast, smooth, and satisfying match-3 action
• 200 levels with Normal, Hard, and Super-Hard challenges
• Powerful striped gems, bombs, rainbow gems, and massive combos
• Diverse objectives, obstacles, layouts, and game modes
• Rewards, records, milestones, cosmetics, and weekly challenges
• Colorful neon visuals, lively effects, and joyful sound
• Easy to learn, rewarding to master

Whether you have a minute to relax or want to chase the perfect combo, Neon Jewels always has another bright challenge waiting. Start matching and make the whole galaxy glow!
